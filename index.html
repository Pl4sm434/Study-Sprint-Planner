    How the Study Sprint Planner scheduling works

    The planner automates student decision-making using an Earliest-Deadline-First (EDF) algorithm with strict Pomodoro slicing:

    • Earliest Deadline First (EDF): Assignments closest to their deadline are scheduled first to prevent last-minute cramming.
    • 25-Minute Sprints: Study time is divided into focused sessions of up to 25 minutes. A 60-minute task is cleanly split into 25m + 25m + 10m blocks.
    • Daily Availability Cap: Today's total scheduled time will never exceed your chosen daily study limit. Excess work flows to tomorrow.
    • Strict Deadline Enforcement: Work is never scheduled after an assignment's due date.
    • Overcapacity Alerts: If the total work required before a deadline exceeds what your daily limits can fit, the app flags it in the warning banner so you know immediately.
